# Project Brief: Nocturne Studio OS

## Product Overview
Nocturne Studio OS is an ultra-premium, high-energy whitelabel SaaS platform designed specifically for the next generation of recording studio owners, music producers, and audio engineers. It transforms traditional studio management into a high-fidelity digital experience.

### Core Value Proposition
"Unshackle Your Studio." Nocturne provides the infrastructure (CRM, scheduling, file management) wrapped in a precision-engineered, customizable brand identity that allows studio owners to operate on their own domains with zero Nocturne branding.

---

## Target Audience
- **Primary:** Tech-forward recording studio owners in Egypt and globally.
- **Secondary:** Independent music producers and audio engineers who require professional, client-facing infrastructure.
- **Persona:** Professionals who value cutting-edge aesthetics, smooth workflows, and "Infrastructure as Aesthetic."

---

## Visual & Brand Identity (Neon Nocturne)
- **Theme:** Ultra-premium dark mode.
- **Color Palette:**
    - Primary: Electric Neon Cyan (#00f2ff)
    - Accents: Cosmic Violet, Acid Lime.
    - Base: Deep Ink Black (#0B0B0C).
- **Typography:** Montserrat (Geometric Sans-Serif).
- **Aesthetic Pillars:**
    - High-tech glassmorphism.
    - 1px neon gradient borders.
    - Floating 3D-perspective UI elements.
    - Kinetic, asymmetric scrollytelling layouts.

---

## Feature Roadmap

### 1. Studio Management (CRM & Scheduler)
- **Multi-Room Matrix:** Conflict-aware scheduling engine for complex studio environments.
- **Artist Portal:** Self-serve booking and mix review interface for roster management.
- **Utilization Intel:** Real-time revenue and occupancy metrics.

### 2. Infrastructure & File Handling
- **Immutable Audio Vault:** Automated session backup directly to private AWS S3 buckets.
- **Whitelabel Engine:** Dynamic domain switching and brand styling (CSS/logo injection) based on custom domains.

### 3. Client Conversion Flow
- **Inquiry System:** Multi-step onboarding (Inquire -> Consult -> Launch).
- **Secure Your Slot:** High-fidelity lead capture form integrated with the CRM.

---

## Technical Specifications (Infrastructure Tiers)
- **Shared Subdomain Tier:** Core CRM features on a `*.nocturne.os` subdomain.
- **Premium Custom Domain Tier:** 
    - Dedicated Database Instance.
    - Custom SSL Routing.
    - Full whitelabeling (zero Nocturne branding).
    - Dedicated infrastructure isolation.
